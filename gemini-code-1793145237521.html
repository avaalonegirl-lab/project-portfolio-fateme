01 / CONTEXT

Project Overview

Rethinking the Student Kitchen

The shared student kitchen is often a space of friction, limited fridge storage, and daily chaos. In this project, we investigated everyday student experiences to explore how experience design can restore order, hygiene, and trust.

02 / DISCOVER

Research & Understanding

How might we reduce friction, food spoilage, and space-sharing challenges in crowded student kitchens?

Through deep interviews and field observations, we mapped peak-hour chaos and found that students primarily need transparent boundaries and quick, reliable organization methods.

03 / EXPLORE

Design Process

From initial restrictions to smart shared interactions.

We tested multiple iterations: starting from fully locked cabinets, moving to semi-transparent glass partitions, and finally integrating digital map reservations.

04 / MAKE & TEST

Outcome & Evaluation

The Proposed Experience

Our final concept combines student-card-enabled smart shelves, transparent dividers, and an interactive digital booking map to transform the kitchen into a calm environment.

📷 [UPLOAD FINAL 3D CONCEPT & SCREENS]
05 / LEARN

Reflection & Next Steps

“The most important thing I learned was that experience design in physical spaces is not just about the look of an object, but about understanding collective behavior and mutual trust.”

I learned how to translate qualitative field research directly into tangible service and interaction specifications. Next step: testing the digital reservation map as a clickable prototype.
